# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Fabric server admins browsing Modrinth/GitHub for a Minecraft-to-Discord chat bridge. Secondary: players on community servers who see the bot in Discord.

## Product Purpose
DMCL is a server-side Fabric mod (MC 1.21.1+) bridging Minecraft chat and Discord at near-native fidelity: webhook-per-player with skin-head avatars, two-way pings, replies, edits, attachments, event embeds, account linking.

## Positioning
Full-fidelity bridge: each player appears in Discord as themselves, not as a single bot.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Server-only mod. MIT licensed. Published on Modrinth (slug `dmcl`) and GitHub Releases. Author handle: n1ght.

## Brand Commitments
Name "DMCL" (uppercase). Identity direction chosen by the user: pixel-grid mark (blocky glyph on a pixel grid). Must not copy Discord or Minecraft logos.

## Evidence on Hand
No existing logo or assets before this work.

## Product Principles
- Utility first: reads as "chat bridge" at 64px.
- Two sides, one link: Minecraft side and Discord side meet in the middle.
- No claims beyond what the mod does.
